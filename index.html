The gap

A doorbell. A smoke alarm. Your name across the room.
A conversation in three languages at once.

For millions of Deaf and hard-of-hearing people, these moments pass by unseen. EverHeard is built to close that gap — not with one feature, but with a sense of the whole room.

Two senses, working together

It listens the way a hearing person does — for speech, and for everything else.

Speech sense

Live multilingual captions

EverHeard captions speech as it happens — in the language it's actually spoken, never machine-translated into something else. English, Spanish, French and more, side by side, detected automatically. It runs entirely on your device.

Sound sense

Environmental sound alerts

Doorbells, knocks, alarms, running water, breaking glass — EverHeard recognizes hundreds of everyday sounds and writes them right into your captions. When it's sure, it names the sound; when it isn't, it's honest — “a rumble,” “a screech” — instead of guessing wrong. Never confidently wrong.

Eyes-free by design

You don't have to be looking

Every alert arrives as a distinct vibration — a doorbell feels different from an alarm feels different from your name — and life-safety sounds flash the screen. Awareness you can feel from across the room, without staring at a phone.

Why it's different

Private by default. Safe by design. Built to last.

On-device & private.

Your audio never leaves your device. No cloud, no accounts, no listening servers — it works offline, and it can't be eavesdropped.

Original language, never translated.

Deaf multilingual users deserve their own words, not an English approximation. EverHeard captions each language as spoken.

Always-on safety.

Sound awareness and captions run in parallel off a single microphone — you never have to choose between the two.

Never confidently wrong.

Honest three-tier labeling names the sounds it's sure of and describes the rest as textures — trust over false precision.

Adapts to any room.

A self-tuning, self-healing microphone adjusts to quiet offices or noisy waiting rooms and recovers on its own — tested in a live hospital waiting room.

Built on open technology.

Proven open, on-device models mean world-class quality at no per-user cost — and we credit every one of them.
