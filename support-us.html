Support EverHeard and the team building it.

EverHeard is free, with no ads and no account, and it processes your audio on your device. Supporting it is absolutely voluntary and never required: the app works exactly the same either way. If EverHeard helps you and you'd like to support it, we truly appreciate it. Every bit helps.

Please note: payments go to BTC Media Labs LLC, the company that builds EverHeard. They are not a donation to a charity and are not tax-deductible. Monthly payments renew each month until you cancel, and you can cancel any time in your PayPal account under automatic payments. PayPal handles the payment under its own privacy policy and shares your payment details with us (such as your name, email and the amount) so we can keep a record; see our Privacy Policy.
