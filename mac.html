What you get

🗨️ Live captions

On-device Whisper turns speech into text — and auto-detects the language, so it captions Spanish, French, and more in their own words.

🪟 Floating bar

A caption bar that stays on top and follows you across every desktop. Drag it anywhere; lock it to click through.

🔔 Sound alerts

A banner and a screen flash when it hears a doorbell, knock, alarm, phone, and more — a whole-screen flash for urgent ones.

🔒 Private by design
