Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

EverHeard is private by design. Everything it hears is processed on your device. Your conversations and the sounds around you never leave your phone — there are no accounts and no servers that receive them.

What EverHeard accesses

EverHeard uses your device's microphone to do two things in real time: turn nearby speech into on-screen captions, and recognize environmental sounds (like a doorbell, alarm, or knock) to alert you.

Everything happens on your device

Speech recognition and sound recognition run entirely on your phone using on-device models. Audio is processed live and is not recorded, stored, or transmitted for these features to work.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, login, or profile to use the core features.
  • We do not send your audio, captions, or detected sounds to any server.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking, and we do not show ads.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone.

Information stored on your device

Your preferences (such as the name you choose to be alerted by and your haptic settings) and any recent transcript history are stored locally on your device only. You can remove all of it at any time by deleting the app.

Network use

The only time EverHeard uses the network is a one-time download of the speech-recognition model the first time you use captions. This downloads data to your device; no conversation data is ever uploaded. After that, captions and sound alerts work fully offline.

Permissions

EverHeard requests microphone access, which is required for captions and sound alerts. You can revoke it any time in your device Settings; the app will simply stop listening.

Children's privacy

EverHeard does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because nothing is collected or transmitted, no personal data leaves the device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and revise the date above.
