EverHeard™.app

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

EverHeard is private by design. EverHeard never sends your conversations or the sounds around you anywhere. There's no account, and everything it hears is processed on your device.

This policy covers EverHeard on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac, and EverHeard for Android, which is in development. Where the platforms differ, it says so.

What EverHeard accesses

EverHeard uses your device's microphone to do two things in real time: turn nearby speech into on-screen captions, and recognize environmental sounds (like a doorbell, alarm, or knock) to alert you. It listens only while captions or sound alerts are turned on.

Everything happens on your device

Speech recognition and sound recognition run entirely on your device using on-device models. Audio is processed live and is not recorded, stored, or transmitted for these features to work.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, login, or profile to use the core features.
  • We do not send your audio, captions, or detected sounds to any server.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking, and we do not show ads. The Android app doesn't use an advertising ID.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone.

Information stored on your device

Your preferences (such as the name you choose to be alerted by and your haptic settings) are stored locally on your device only. If you choose to save conversations, EverHeard keeps the caption text (never audio) on your device. You can delete saved conversations one by one in History, or clear them all when you turn saving off. On iPhone, iPad and Android, deleting the app removes everything EverHeard stored.

  • iPhone and iPad: saving conversations is off by default; turn it on in Settings. If you used version 1.0, where saving was on by default, your setting carries over; you can turn it off in Settings. Saved conversations are excluded from iCloud and computer backups.
  • Mac: saving is off by default. If you turn it on, conversations are saved to a folder you choose. If that folder is included in your backups or synced to a cloud service such as iCloud Drive, copies may be stored there under your own backup and sync settings. That's your choice, not something EverHeard does, and we can't access those copies. You delete saved conversations in that folder ("Open saved conversations…" in the menu). Moving the app to the Trash doesn't remove them, or the settings EverHeard keeps in your Library folder.
  • Android: saving conversations is off by default. On Android, saved conversations are excluded from Google backups and from transfers to a new phone.

Wherever you choose to keep saved conversations or copies of them (a folder on a computer, a cloud sync service, a file you export or share, or an Android emulator on a computer that is itself backed up), those copies follow your own settings and choices. EverHeard doesn't send them anywhere, and we can't access them.

Your voice profile (optional)

On iPhone, iPad and Mac, speaker colours can tell your voice apart from others. Only if you choose "This is my voice", EverHeard listens to about five seconds of your speech and turns it into a set of numbers (a voice profile) stored on your device. No audio is kept, and the profile never leaves your device: it's excluded from iCloud, computer and Time Machine backups. You can delete it any time with "Forget my voice" in Settings (iPhone and iPad) or in the menu (Mac). On iPhone and iPad, deleting the app also removes it; on Mac it stays until you choose "Forget my voice". There is no voice profile on Android or Apple Watch.

Network use

EverHeard uses the network only to download its models, once, and when you tap a link to everheard.app:

  • Captions: the speech-recognition model is downloaded from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) the first time you use captions.
  • Speaker colours (iPhone, iPad and Mac): a speaker-recognition model is downloaded from GitHub (github.com) the first time speaker colours are used. It's a general model file, not anything about you.

These downloads bring data to your device; no conversation, audio or caption is ever uploaded. They send no account, advertising or device identifier. Like any download, the server sees your IP address and basic app and operating-system information. After the downloads, captions and sound alerts work offline.

Permissions

EverHeard requests microphone access, which is required for captions and sound alerts. On Android it also uses vibration for alerts, and the internet only for the model download. You can revoke microphone access any time in your device settings; the app will simply stop listening.

When you contact us or choose to support EverHeard

The app itself sends us nothing. We only receive information you choose to send us outside the app:

  • Email and sharing. If you choose to email us or share a conversation, it's sent through your own email or sharing app, and we receive only what you send. We use your message and email address only to reply and to improve EverHeard.
  • Optional payments. If you choose to support EverHeard on our website, PayPal handles the payment under its own privacy policy and shares your payment details with us (such as your name, email and the amount) so BTC Media Labs LLC can keep the records the law requires.
  • Beta testing. If you join the beta, Apple's TestFlight or Google Play shares your tester email with us so we can invite you.

We never sell or share this information. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted where the law allows, write to support@everheard.app.

Children's privacy

EverHeard does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because nothing is collected or transmitted, no personal data leaves the device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and revise the date above.
